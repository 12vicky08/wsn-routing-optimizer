## Corpus-level observations

- Of the 18 substantively-extracted WSN papers, only **5** address mobile-sink trajectory/path planning (Hamidouche MSSA, Khalily-Dermany OP_Sink/MA_Sink, Mohan MFO-MS, Sangeetha ExAq-MSPP, and the abstract-only OptiGACHS) — the rest are static-sink GA-based clustering/routing/coverage papers. This matters directly for how thematically our related work should be organized (see research-gap.md).
- None of the 18 papers report a **traffic-load sensitivity sweep** (offered load vs. metric) as a distinct experiment — all vary node count, area, or algorithm parameters instead.
- Simulators used across the corpus: NS2 (5 papers), MATLAB (6 papers), OMNeT++ (2 papers), WSNSimPy (1), custom/unspecified (4). **None use ns-3** — our ns-3.45-based evaluation is a methodological point of difference worth noting, not just a detail.
- Energy models: most use the Heinzelman et al. first-order radio model (free-space/multipath threshold), consistent with wsn-metrics skill definitions. A few (Cui UAV paper, Mohan MFO-MS) use non-radio energy proxies.
