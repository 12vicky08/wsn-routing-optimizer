## 10. New from real data: network lifetime (FND/HND) doesn't differentiate schemes; single-hop-only is a scope limitation

**Not in the original list, found during the real sweep.** Two honest limitations to disclose in the paper rather than hide:
- FND/HND are statistically flat across all six schemes (see #2) — real WSN energy consumption at this radio/traffic/duty-cycle configuration is idle-listening-dominated well before trajectory quality has time to matter. LND does differentiate (CGAPD clearly worst; the other five overlap). The Results section should report LND, not FND, as the lifetime metric, and should not claim IAGAPC-Enhanced has the best lifetime — the honest claim is "competitive with adaptive-GA baselines, clearly ahead of rigid clustering."
- The simulation is single-hop only (every sensor sends directly to the sink) — no multi-hop relaying, despite the draft's system model (Section 3.1) describing multi-hop communication to reach the sink or a rendezvous point. This is why absolute PDR/throughput are low (single-digit % PDR) across every scheme: with ~70–100m ranges over a 1000×1000m field and no relaying, most nodes are simply out of direct range most of the time. This should be stated as a scope limitation in Results/Discussion or Limitations, not silently glossed over — the six-way scheme comparison is still valid (identical harness, identical limitation applied to all), but the absolute numbers shouldn't be presented as representative of a full multi-hop deployment.
